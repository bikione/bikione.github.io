Leadership

How I lead teams and projects

I have led projects in every role since 2018. These are the habits I bring to every team.

Make the plan visible

Clear milestones, named owners and one place to see progress. People do their best work when they know what done looks like.

Grow the people around me

I mentor through real projects and code review, so juniors leave a project able to own the next one.

Pay down risk early

Legacy code, fragile integrations and unclear environments slow every team. I fix the foundations so features ship safely.

Tie the work to the business

I explain technical work in terms of hours saved, calls avoided and money kept, which is how projects get funded and adopted.
